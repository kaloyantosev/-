I. DEFINITIONS

Art. 1. (1) For the purposes of this Agreement, the terms listed below shall have the following meaning, unless the context requires otherwise:

- "Services" means the Payment initiation service within the meaning of Art. 4, item 7 of the PSPSA and Account information service, within the meaning of Art. 4, item 8 of the PSPSA.

- "Payment initiation service" is an electronic payment service, in which at the request of the Merchant (with the explicit consent of the Customer, realized as per the implemented procedure of the payment service provider to service the Customer’s accounts) a payment is initiated (a payment order is generated and executed) from the Customer’s account in a bank/payment institution to the Merchant's bank/payment account;

- "Account information service" is an electronic payment service in which the Merchant receives information on the balances and movements on the payment accounts opened by him in banks/payment institutions;

- "The Product" or "Payment terminal" is a software solution provided by the Provider, through which the Merchant can initiate payments to their Customers.

- "Customer" is any natural person or legal entity that wishes to pay through the technology realized by the Payment Terminal for goods/services of the Merchant.

(2) Unless the context suggests otherwise, the words used in singular form mean the plural form of these words, and the words used in plural form, mean the same words in singular form.

II. SUBJECT

Art. 2. (1) The following Agreement regulates the provision of the Services through the Products.

(2) The Merchant’s use of the Account information service for one or more payment accounts, registered at one or more payment service providers includes:
a) Information on the list of payment accounts;
b) Information on payment account balances;
c) Details and movements on payment accounts.

(3) Payments between Customers and the Merchant will be made through Products after successful registration and identification of the Merchant and integration with the payment system of the Provider, depending on whether it is paid at an online/physical store or by document/invoice.

(4) The Provider is a payment intermediary, but is not a party in the legal relationship between the Merchant and his customers, related to the trade activities performed by the Merchant. The Provider is not liable for abuse and harmful actions performed by Customers, as well as for errors or problems on the part of the banks/payment institutions servicing the respective accounts of the Merchant and/or Customers.

III. PROVISION OF SERVICES

Art. 3. (1) The provision of the Account Information service is performed in accordance with the standards implemented by the service provider(s) of the account, whose client is the Merchant.

(2) Payment through the Payment initiation service is related to the execution of payment transactions to an account specified by the Merchant. The transfer operation itself is performed by the bank/payment institution which services the Customer's account, and the Provider is not liable for its implementation, as long as he has provided a timely proper payment order to the bank/payment institution.

I. DEFINIȚII

Art. 1. (1) În sensul prezentului Acord, termenii enumerați mai jos vor avea următorul înțeles, cu excepția cazului în care contextul cere altfel:

- „Serviciul de inițiere a plății” este un serviciu de plată electronică, în care la solicitarea Comerciantului (cu acordul explicit al Clientului, realizat conform procedurii implementate de furnizorul de servicii de plată pentru deservirea conturilor Clientului) se inițiază o plată (se generează și se execută un ordin de plată) din contul Clientului într-o bancă/instituție de plată către banca/contul de plată al Comerciantului;

- „Serviciul de informare cu privire la cont” este un serviciu de plată electronică în care Comerciantul primește informații despre soldurile și mișcările din conturile de plăți deschise de acesta în bănci/instituții de plată;

- „Produsul” sau „Terminalul de plată” este o soluție software furnizată de Furnizor, prin care Comerciantul poate iniția plăți către Clienții săi.

- „Client” este orice persoană fizică sau juridică ce dorește să plătească prin tehnologia realizată de Terminalul de Plată pentru bunuri/servicii ale Comerciantului.

(2) Cu excepția cazului în care contextul sugerează altfel, cuvintele folosite la singular înseamnă și forma de plural a acestor cuvinte, iar cuvintele folosite la forma plural înseamnă aceleași cuvinte la singular.

II. OBIECTUL
